1. Ancient Vedic Origins of Vijaya (Cannabis Sativa L.)

For more than three millennia, Vijaya has occupied a revered position within classical Ayurvedic pharmacopoeia. Documented extensively across foundational texts including the Charaka Samhita, Sushruta Samhita, and Bhavaprakasha, Vijaya is celebrated as one of the five sacred plants in the Atharva Veda.

In traditional Ayurvedic energetics, Vijaya exhibits Tikta (bitter) and Kashaya (astringent) tastes, with Ushna (warming) potency and Laghu (light) qualities. It acts as an efficacious Deepana (digestive stimulant), Vedanasthapana (analgesic/pain pacifier), and Nidrajanana (promoter of restorative sleep), principally balancing aggravated Vata and Kapha doshas.

🌿 Ayurvedic Classical Reference

“Vijaya enhances internal digestive fire (Jatharagni), calms ungrounded vata fluctuations, and restores tranquility to the disturbed mind without cognitive dullness when purified according to Shodhana protocols.” — Raja Nighantu

2. The Endocannabinoid Regulatory System (ECS)

Modern neurobiology validates ancient Ayurvedic wisdom through the discovery of the Endocannabinoid System (ECS) — the central biological homeostatic regulator found throughout mammalian physiology. The ECS governs nociception (pain perception), inflammatory cascade management, sleep architecture, immune homeostasis, and emotional equilibrium.

Phytocannabinoids synthesized naturally in the trichomes of the Vijaya leaf act as direct botanical mimetics for endogenous cannabinoids such as Anandamide and 2-Arachidonoylglycerol (2-AG). By interacting selectively with CB1 receptors in the central nervous system and CB2 receptors throughout the peripheral immune cells, Sanan Relief formulations restore biological balance without intoxicating sedation.

3. Pristine Single-Origin Himalayan Sourcing

Therapeutic efficacy begins with terroir. Sanan Relief harvests exclusively from indigenous landrace cannabis strains ethically cultivated across designated high-altitude valleys in Uttarakhand and Himachal Pradesh. These pristine alpine microclimates, nourished by glacial runoff and mineral-rich mountain soil, naturally produce unprecedented terpene concentrations and robust secondary cannabinoid profiles.

Our cooperative farming partners adhere strictly to chemical-free, regenerative agricultural practices. No synthetic fertilizers, chemical pesticides, or heavy metal stimulants are ever utilized during cultivation.

6. Ministry of AYUSH Schedule E-1 Legal Framework

Under the Drugs and Cosmetics Act (1940) and Drugs and Cosmetics Rules (1945), Vijaya (Cannabis sativa leaf) is classified as an authentic Classical Ayurvedic ingredient listed under Schedule E-1. Formulations produced by licensed Ayurvedic manufacturers are 100% legal for medical dispensing across India when authorized by a registered medical practitioner.
