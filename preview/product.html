Four versatile administration methods calibrated for maximum cellular bioavailability and personalized relief.

Sublingually

Place 2–4 drops directly under your tongue. Hold for 60–90 seconds before swallowing for direct mucosal absorption into the bloodstream within 15–20 minutes.

Direct drops

Take drops directly on an empty stomach or 20 minutes before meals for smooth digestion, steady hepatic conversion, and prolonged 4–6 hour restorative comfort.

With a drink

Stir drops into your morning green tea, matcha latte, warm golden milk, or evening chamomile. The organic MCT carrier blends seamlessly without altering flavor.

Topically

Massage 3–5 drops directly onto sore muscles, stiff neck, knees, elbows, or arthritic joints. Phytocannabinoids penetrate localized CB2 receptors for fast soothing relief.
