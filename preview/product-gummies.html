Edible Pharmacokinetics

How does it work?

Uplift Gummies feature a two-phase absorption mechanism combining oral mucosal pre-absorption with prolonged hepatic endocannabinoid modulation.

As you chew the fruit pectin matrix for 60 seconds, micro-droplets of active cannabinoids absorb directly through oral mucosal capillaries, providing early calming signals within 15 to 25 minutes.
Swallowed actives travel to the liver where first-pass metabolism converts THC into 11-hydroxy-THC, a metabolite known for deeper, more enveloping somatic relaxation and sustained 6-8 hour relief.
Phytocannabinoids bind with peripheral CB2 immune receptors to calm bodily inflammation and central CB1 brain receptors to quieten racing thoughts, lower cortisol, and foster peaceful restorative comfort.
Formulated strictly following Ayush classical guidelines. Helps restore your natural sleep-wake circadian rhythm and nervous equilibrium without synthetic sedation or groggy morning hangovers.
Simple Daily Rituals

How do I use it?

Follow these 4 simple steps to maximize your recreational enjoyment, personalized dosage titration, and prolonged wellness comfort.

1. Start with half

If you are new to full-spectrum edible Vatis, cut 1 gummy in half (approx. 200mg/150mg active). Assess your baseline comfort and observe how your body responds.

2. Chew slowly

Savor the real fruit flavour and chew mindfully for 45–60 seconds before swallowing. This activates buccal sublingual pathways for quicker, smoother onset.

3. Hydrate & wait

Drink a tall glass of water or warm chamomile tea. Edible cannabinoids take 30 to 60 minutes to metabolize fully into systemic bloodstream circulation.

4. Feel the shift

Sink into restorative evening sleep, joyful social warmth, or localized muscle ease as the active botanical matrix supports your mind and body for 6–8 hours.
